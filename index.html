My Projects

01. GlowKart – Personalized Beauty Product Recommendation System

GlowKart is an online beauty shopping website that helps customers discover beauty products based on their skin type. Users can select Oily, Dry, Sensitive or Combination skin and explore products suitable for their selection.

Key Features:

  • User registration and login.
  • Skin-type-based product discovery.
  • Product search and product details.
  • Wishlist and shopping cart management.
  • User profile and order management.
  • Admin dashboard for managing products, users and orders.
  • Dark and light theme options.

Technologies: HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, XAMPP and phpMyAdmin.

02. FOODTRUCK – Smart Food Delivery & Logistics Management System

FOODTRUCK is an Android-based food delivery and logistics application that connects customers, restaurants and delivery riders through a unified platform. Customers can browse restaurants, order food and track deliveries in real time. Restaurants can manage menus and incoming orders, while riders can accept delivery requests, navigate using GPS and update delivery status.

Key Features:

  • Customer food ordering and live delivery tracking.
  • Restaurant menu and order management.
  • Rider delivery requests and GPS navigation.
  • Admin dashboard for managing the platform.
  • Smart rider allocation and batch delivery optimization.
  • Real-time order and location updates.

Technologies: Java, XML, Android Studio, Python FastAPI, SQLite, PostgreSQL/MySQL, Firebase, Google Maps SDK, Google Location API, WebSockets and Retrofit.
